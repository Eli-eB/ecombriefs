A narrow scope, examined properly

A diagnostic, not a data dump

The document is designed to move from evidence to an implementation task without making your developer repeat the investigation.

Included

  • Evidence for every barrier: URL, CSS selector, HTML, rule and impact.
  • Findings grouped by root cause, rather than repeated as a long list.
  • Pages and instances affected, so the scope of a fix is visible.
  • Confirmed and indeterminate cases separated, so uncertainty is never sold as fact.

Not included

  • No invented score or arbitrary mark out of 100.
  • No legal certification or promise of compliance.
  • No promise of higher sales from a technical finding.
  • No remediation lock-in: your own team or agency can fix it.
